What this is. Each page follows one family or line of people through Utah's public records: elected office, lobbying licenses, board and party posts, staff jobs, and money between them. Everything shown as confirmed has a record behind it. Anything we've been told but can't yet document is labeled Being checked until it is.

How we read the records

  • Family ties come from obituaries, official biographies, news reports and other public records.
  • Lobbying ties come from the state lobbyist register (lobbyist.utah.gov).
  • Money comes from campaign finance filings (disclosures.utah.gov).
  • Only people with public roles are profiled. Relatives appear only where a record ties them to a public role.
