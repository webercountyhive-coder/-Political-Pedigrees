The Gibson Family

Who held office, who lobbied, who ran a PAC, and how the family ties connect in the public record. Weber County.
Political Pedigrees › The Gibson Family

Posted: Nov. 13, 2026 · Updated: Nov. 13, 2026

New to this? Read it in plain words About 500 words.
The short version. The Gibsons of West Weber have run a dairy farm in Weber County for about 150 years, and three generations have held public posts. Wayne B. Gibson (1937–2021) served on the Weber Basin Water District board for nearly 30 years and on the Weber County Planning Commission. His son Kerry Gibson served in the Utah House, on the Weber County Commission, as deputy director of the Department of Natural Resources and as state agriculture commissioner, and has been a registered lobbyist since 2021. Another son, Ron Gibson, was president of the Utah Farm Bureau from 2015 to 2023. State Rep. Ryan Wilcox is Wayne's grandson, the son of Wayne's daughter Melanie and her husband Roy Wilcox. Kerry's wife, Katrina Gibson, ran for the Weber County Commission in 2026 and is tied to Utah Forward PAC, which gave to Ryan Wilcox's campaign and paid a recipient listed as Steven Gibson. Pleasant View Mayor Steve Gibson, a city councilman for 12 years before that, is married to Dana Beesley Gibson, the sister of Jon Beesley, the former Plain City mayor who takes a seat on the Weber County Commission in January. Steve is Kerry's cousin; Kerry called him "cuz" in his own Facebook post (screenshot on file).

Key facts

  • Three generations in public posts: Wayne B. Gibson (Weber Basin Water board, county Planning Commission), his son Kerry Gibson (Utah House, Weber County Commission, two state agencies) and his grandson Rep. Ryan Wilcox (Utah House).
  • From office to lobbying: Kerry Gibson left the agriculture department in January 2020 to run for Congress, lost the primary, and registered as a lobbyist in 2021. His clients include Ogden City, Plain City and Morgan County.
  • A second family lobbyist: Katrina Gibson was registered as a lobbyist under Legislative Solutions from Jan. 10, 2023, until her license was canceled Jan. 14, 2026, six days after she filed to run for County Commission (the Hive's June 2026 review of the register). The Hive's Oct. 27, 2026 register copy shows her client registrations for five of Kerry's clients, including Plain City, from Feb. 4 to April 12, 2025. (Earlier wording, kept: "The state register shows Katrina Gibson was also registered as a lobbyist from Feb. 4 to April 12, 2025, for five of Kerry's clients, including Plain City.")
  • Kerry signed, then Katrina replaced him: The May 27, 2026 court petition to remove James Ebert from the ballot was first signed by Kerry Gibson, who wasn't a party to the case. A version with Katrina Gibson's signature replaced his the next day. Both versions are in the court record.
  • PAC contribution to Wilcox; payment to Steven Gibson: Utah Forward PAC, LLC, with Katrina Gibson as its registered agent, gave $3,000 to Rep. Ryan Wilcox's campaign in January 2026 and paid $1,000 to a recipient listed as Steven Gibson in July 2025, per state filings.
  • Plain City: Kerry Gibson was registered to lobby for Plain City while Jon Beesley was its mayor, and Plain City paid $42,000 for his firm's services, signed off by Beesley on May 15, 2025. Beesley won the 2026 Commission Seat B primary and had no challenger in the Nov. 3 general election (The Ogdenite, June 2026).
  • Ferry crossover: Kerry Gibson was DNR deputy director before Joel Ferry ran DNR, and Joel's campaign gave Friends of Kerry Gibson $1,000 in 2020. See the Ferry family page.

Who's who

Wayne B. Gibson (1937–2021)

Dairy farmer who owned Gibson's Green Acres Dairy on the family's 150-year-old farm in West Weber. Served on the Weber Basin Water District board for nearly 30 years, as president several times; on the Weber County Planning Commission; and as Weber County Farm Bureau president. His obituary says President Reagan appointed him to the State ASC Committee. Died Dec. 4, 2021. His obituary names daughters Melanie (Roy) Wilcox, D'niece (Kevin) Davis, Lynnette (Alan) Thurber and Evonne (Bill) Howard, and sons Kerry (Katrina) Gibson and Ron (Andrea) Gibson, with 30 grandchildren.

Kerry Gibson

Wayne's son. Utah House District 6 (2005–2011, per Ballotpedia); Weber County Commission (January 2011 to January 2018); deputy director of the Department of Natural Resources (announced December 2017; Utah News Dispatch reports his start was delayed and he served June 2018 to April 2019); Utah Commissioner of Agriculture and Food (mid-April 2019 to Jan. 14, 2020). Resigned to run for Congress and lost the June 30, 2020 Republican primary in the 1st District. Registered as a lobbyist Jan. 17, 2021, through his firm. A criminal investigation of his time on the County Commission, from fall 2017 to May 2018, ended with no charges, according to the Utah Investigative Journalism Project.

Katrina Gibson

Kerry's wife. Registered agent of Utah Forward PAC, LLC (state business filing, formed April 13, 2022). Registered as a lobbyist under Legislative Solutions from Jan. 10, 2023, to Jan. 14, 2026, when her license was canceled, six days after she filed for County Commission on Jan. 8, 2026 (the Hive's June review). The Oct. 27, 2026 register copy shows her registered Feb. 4 to April 12, 2025, for Plain City, Morgan County, Mountain Green Sewer Improvement District, Athenian eAcademy and the Coalition of Utah Autism Providers, all clients Kerry also represents. PAC filings list her as the PAC's chief financial officer and treasurer. On her 2026 conflict-of-interest form she listed her spouse's employer as "N/A" (the Hive, June 2026). Ran for Weber County Commission Seat A in 2026 and finished third in the June 23 primary with 25.22% (The Ogdenite). Manages the Korner Fix diner, per Utah News Dispatch.

Ron Gibson

Wayne's son and Kerry's brother. Elected Utah Farm Bureau president in 2015 (Utah Policy) and served until he resigned in August 2023 (Salt Lake Tribune). Before that he was Weber County Farm Bureau president. No lobbying registration found for him in the Hive's register copy.

Rep. Ryan Wilcox

Son of Melanie Gibson Wilcox and Roy Wilcox, so Wayne's grandson and Kerry's nephew (confirmed to the Hive by a family member in June 2026; the obituaries show Melanie married Roy Wilcox). Served in the Utah House from January 2009 to June 2014, then as northern Utah director for U.S. Sen. Mike Lee, and was elected again in House District 7 in 2020 (Ballotpedia; his campaign biography). His campaign took $3,000 from Utah Forward PAC in January 2026, and he and his wife gave $500 to Katrina Gibson's campaign that month, per state filings.

Mayor Steve Gibson

Sworn in as Pleasant View mayor on Jan. 13, 2026, after 12 years on the city council (Pleasant View council minutes; his state voter-guide entry, which also lists 12 years on the Weber County Mosquito Abatement board). That night the council appointed him to a four-year term on the Central Weber Sewer Improvement District board, 5-0; Councilman Johnny Ferry seconded the motion. His wife is Dana Beesley Gibson, as he wrote in a public Facebook comment ("Both Dana Beesley Gibson and I voted"), and city minutes show the two of them presenting the Neighbors in Action Committee's awards. Utah Forward PAC paid a recipient listed as "Steven Gibson" $1,000 on July 29, 2025, listed as "Donations"; the filing doesn't say whether that's the mayor. He is Kerry Gibson's cousin: Kerry wished him "Happy Birthday cuz" in his own Facebook post, which was public when the Hive saw it (screenshot on file). Confirmed

Jon Beesley

Former Plain City mayor (2018 to January 2026) and Weber County commissioner-elect. As mayor he also sat on the Bona Vista Water board, per Weber County's taxing-district list. Won the Weber County Commission Seat B primary on June 23, 2026, with 55.57%, and had no challenger in the Nov. 3 general election (The Ogdenite), so he takes office in January 2027. Kerry Gibson was registered to lobby for Plain City while he was mayor. Beesley, Katrina Gibson and Richard Hyer sued together in May 2026 to remove James Ebert from the Seat A ballot; Kerry Gibson signed the first version of the petition, and the judge denied it. As mayor, Beesley signed off on $42,000 for Kerry Gibson's firm on May 15, 2025. His own Plain City biography says he was born in 1975 to James and Carol Jean Beesley, the youngest of four children with two older sisters and one older brother. Plain City's Jan. 15, 2026 minutes list "Jim and Carol Jean Beesley" among those present when the council honored him, and record Carol Jean Beesley speaking as his mother. Mayor Steve Gibson's wife is Dana Beesley Gibson (his own public Facebook comment), and she is one of Jon's two older sisters, so Steve Gibson is Jon's brother-in-law (confirmed by the Hive). Confirmed Being checked His father, Judge James L. Beesley, a former Plain City councilman, was appointed to the Plain City Justice Court in July 1988 and also served as Harrisville's justice court judge; the Utah courts now list him as an inactive senior judge (Utah Judicial Performance Evaluation Commission; Utah State Courts).

Where the Gibsons and the Ferrys meet

  • The same department: Kerry Gibson was DNR deputy director from 2018 to April 2019. Joel Ferry ran DNR from 2022 to 2026.
  • Campaign money: Joel Ferry's House campaign gave Friends of Kerry Gibson $1,000 on April 7, 2020, during Gibson's congressional run (Joel Ferry's 2020 campaign report).
  • Weber Basin Water: Wayne Gibson served on the Weber Basin Water District board for nearly 30 years. David Stewart has been registered to lobby for Weber Basin Water Conservancy District since Dec. 18, 2014. Wayne's years on the board aren't stated, so whether they overlapped isn't known.
  • Pleasant View City Hall: Johnny Ferry, whose campaign report thanks "Mom" Teresa Ferry (see the Ferry page), was sworn in to the Pleasant View City Council on Jan. 13, 2026, the same night Steve Gibson was sworn in as mayor. He seconded the motion appointing Mayor Gibson to the Central Weber Sewer Improvement District board (council minutes).
  • Morgan County: Kerry Gibson and David Stewart were both registered for Morgan County from 2023 to 2024.
  • Medical cannabis grow licenses (context, not a Ferry tie): As agriculture commissioner, Gibson oversaw the 2019 licensing, and three of the eight grow sites are in Box Elder County, including one in Corinne. No record found ties the Ferry family to those licenses.

Words to know

  • Lobbyist: someone paid by outside clients (companies, cities, groups) to talk with lawmakers and officials for them. Utah lobbyists must register each client with the state.
  • Registration: the state record that a lobbyist works for a client, with start and end dates.
  • PAC: a political action committee, a group that raises money and gives it to candidates or causes. It has to report what comes in and what goes out.
  • Registered agent: the person a business names to receive legal papers for it.
  • Commissioner-elect: someone who has won a seat but hasn't taken office yet.
  • Conservancy district: a local government that delivers water, run by an appointed board.

Common questions

Is it against the rules for relatives to lobby, or for a PAC to give to relatives?

No Utah rule found bars either. Utah law requires lobbyists to register and PACs to report what they take in and pay out. This page shows the family ties so readers can weigh them.

Why are some ties marked "being checked"?

We only call a tie confirmed when a public record states it. Ties we've been told about, or that point one way but aren't stated outright, stay labeled until a record backs them up.

Did Jon Beesley do anything for the Gibsons as mayor?

No record found shows a vote or decision made because of a family tie. The records show Kerry Gibson was registered to lobby for Plain City while Beesley was mayor. Whether Beesley is related to the Gibsons is still being checked.
