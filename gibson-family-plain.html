The Gibson Family, in plain words

Who they are

The Gibsons have farmed in West Weber for about 150 years. Wayne B. Gibson, who died in 2021, ran a dairy and spent nearly 30 years on the board of Weber Basin Water, a big water district for northern Utah. He also sat on the county Planning Commission.

His son Kerry Gibson has held a lot of public jobs: state representative, Weber County commissioner, second-in-command at the Department of Natural Resources, and head of the state agriculture department. Another son, Ron, led the Utah Farm Bureau from 2015 to 2023. Wayne's grandson Ryan Wilcox, the son of his daughter Melanie, is a state representative.

From office to lobbying

A lobbyist is someone paid by cities, companies or groups to talk with officials for them. Kerry Gibson left the agriculture department in 2020 to run for Congress. He lost, and in 2021 he became a lobbyist. His clients include Ogden City, Plain City and Morgan County. His wife, Katrina, was also a registered lobbyist under his firm from 2023 until January 2026, when she ran for County Commission. When she and two other candidates went to court to try to take a rival off the ballot, Kerry signed the first version of the court papers; Katrina's signature replaced his the next day.

The PAC

A PAC is a group that collects money and gives it to candidates. Katrina Gibson is the official contact for Utah Forward PAC. State filings show the PAC gave $3,000 to the campaign of Rep. Ryan Wilcox, Wayne's grandson and Kerry's nephew, and paid $1,000 to someone listed as Steven Gibson. Katrina ran for Weber County Commission in 2026 and came in third.

More family in office

Steve Gibson became Pleasant View's mayor in January, after 12 years on its city council. He is Kerry's cousin; Kerry once wished him a happy birthday on Facebook, calling him "cuz." Steve's wife is Dana Beesley Gibson, the sister of Jon Beesley. Beesley was Plain City's mayor while Kerry lobbied for the city, and he joins the Weber County Commission in January. His father, James Beesley, was the justice court judge for Plain City and Harrisville.

Where they meet the Ferrys

Kerry Gibson worked at the Department of Natural Resources a few years before Joel Ferry ran it. Joel's campaign gave $1,000 to Kerry's congressional campaign in 2020. And Johnny Ferry joined the Pleasant View City Council the same night Steve Gibson became mayor.

Questions you can ask

  • How much has Plain City paid Kerry Gibson's firm? Ask the Plain City recorder.
  • Will Jon Beesley step back from county votes that involve Gibson clients? Watch the Weber County Commission agendas.

Words to know

  • Lobbyist: a person paid to speak to officials for someone else.
  • PAC: a group that raises money and gives it to candidates.
  • Commissioner-elect: someone who won a seat but hasn't started yet.
  • Being checked: something we've been told but haven't found in a public record yet.
